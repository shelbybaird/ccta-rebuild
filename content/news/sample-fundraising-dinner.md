The Association's annual fundraising dinner returns on **Saturday 7 November** at
the Union Township Civic Center, from six until nine in the evening. Proceeds
support the Association's scholarship and training programs for township
officials across Clermont County.

Tickets are $35 each, or $250 for a table of eight. **Reservations close on
30 October** and the hall has been full for the last three years, so early
reservations are advised.

## How to reserve

Download the reservation form below, complete it, and return it with payment to
the Treasurer. Townships reserving a full table are asked to send the names of
those attending so that place cards can be prepared.

[Reserve a place](/uploads/SAMPLE-reservation-form.pdf) or
[email the Treasurer](mailto:treasurer@cctownship.org) with any question about
the evening.

## Sponsorship

Businesses and townships wishing to sponsor the evening should contact the
Executive Committee-person. Sponsors are named in the program and on the
Association's website for the year following the dinner.
