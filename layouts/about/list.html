The purpose of the Clermont County Township Association is to protect the township against any attempt to abolish it as a governmental unit, resist efforts to further centralize governmental powers by depriving the township of any rights, duties, or privileges, which it now possesses, and secure an equitable share of gasoline tax revenue.
