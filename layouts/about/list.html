The Clermont County Township Association exists to defend township government in Clermont County and to support the officials who run it.

Article IV of the constitution sets out its purposes: to protect the township as a unit of government, and to resist any effort to strip it of rights, duties or privileges it already holds; to bring township officials into regular contact with one another; to build a better working knowledge of what township office requires, by comparing experience and exchanging ideas; to seek legislation from the General Assembly that allows township government to work more effectively; and to secure for Ohio's township roads an equitable share of gasoline tax revenue, to be spent on those roads by township officials.
