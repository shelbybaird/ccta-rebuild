{{/* Read a front-matter field as a moment in time. Hugo parses `date`, `publishDate`, `expiryDate` and `lastmod` into times of its own accord and leaves every other field as whatever the file's format made of it — which for a date typed into a field of our own is sometimes a string and sometimes already a time, depending on the format and on how the value was quoted when it was saved. A template that assumes either one breaks on the other, and it breaks at build time on a file somebody has just edited, which is the worst moment for it. */}} {{- $v := . -}} {{- if eq (printf "%T" $v) "string" -}}{{- $v = time.AsTime $v "America/New_York" -}}{{- end -}} {{- return $v -}}
