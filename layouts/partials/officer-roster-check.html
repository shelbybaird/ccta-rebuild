{{/* An office is held by one person at a time. Nothing in the editing tool can enforce that, because every check it offers looks at one entry on its own and cannot see that another entry already claims the office. So the rule is enforced here, where the whole roster is in view at once. A duplicate stops the build rather than publishing. The reasoning is that a roster naming two Presidents is read by trustees and fiscal officers who will believe it, whereas a build that stops republishes nothing and leaves yesterday's correct page in place — and the footer's build date makes a site that has stopped updating visible to anyone who looks. Takes a dict of `offices` and `serving`. */}} {{ $offices := .offices }} {{ $serving := .serving }} {{ range $offices }} {{ $office := . }} {{ $holders := where $serving "Params.office" $office }} {{ if gt (len $holders) 1 }} {{ $names := slice }} {{ range $holders }}{{ $names = $names | append .Params.name }}{{ end }} {{ errorf "The roster records %d people as currently serving as %s: %s. An office is held by one person at a time, so the term that has finished needs an end date. Officer entries are files in content/officers/, and the entry to change is the one whose term is over — giving it a Term ended date keeps it in the record instead of deleting it." (len $holders) $office (delimit $names ", " " and ") }} {{ end }} {{ end }}
