An announcement can carry the same words, the same picture and the same files — it was tried, on 5 September 2026, with a fundraising dinner. Two things went wrong and neither is fixable inside an announcement. The flyer filled the first screen, so a reader met a poster and had to scroll past all of it to learn anything. And the two calls to action, being ordinary Markdown links in a paragraph, read as sentence links rather than as the things a reader is being asked to do. So the difference between the two types is not what they can hold. It is that an announcement is a notice in a list, ordered by date and read in passing, and this is a page somebody is sent to. The layout below follows from that: what is being asked for sits at the top beside the picture, at a size that leaves room for words, and the picture is capped so that it illustrates the page instead of becoming it. ⚠ TEMPORARY IS THE DISPLAY WINDOW, not a separate mechanism. `publishDate` and `expiryDate` are the same two fields every announcement has, and Hugo drops a page outside its window from the build entirely — which is why such a page also leaves the menu by itself, with nothing to remember and nothing to undo. */}} {{ $cta := .Params.callsToAction }}
